# Hourly Wage Calculator UK — Hourly Rate to Annual Salary

> £15 an hour at 37.5 hours a week is £29,250 a year, leaving £24,579.60 after tax and National Insurance.

Reference notes for the UK tax year **2026/27**, computed from HMRC's
published rates rather than copied from another site.

## How it works

```
hourly rate × hours per week × 52 = annual salary
```

| Hourly rate | Gross a year | Take-home |
|---|---:|---:|
| £12.71 | £24,784.50 | £21,364.44 |
| £15.00 | £29,250.00 | £24,579.60 |
| £20.00 | £39,000.00 | £31,599.60 |
| £25.00 | £48,750.00 | £38,619.60 |

All at 37.5 hours a week. Change the hours and every row changes.

## What this does not cover

Multiplying by 52 assumes you are paid for holiday, which is true for salaried staff and false for anyone paid only for hours worked. A casual worker taking four weeks off is paid for 48 weeks, so 52 overstates the year by about 8%.

## Source and tax year

Figures are for the 2026/27 tax year, which runs 6 April 2026
to 5 April 2027, and were checked against
[gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) on 28 August 2026.

Rates change every April, and often at a Budget. Check the tax year a figure
belongs to before relying on it.
